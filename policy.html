01

Shipping & Delivery

We deliver all over Bangladesh through trusted courier partners. Every order is confirmed by phone before it is shipped.

AreaDelivery timeCharge

  • Delivery time starts after your order is confirmed by phone.
  • Some bundle offers (for example “Buy 3”) include free delivery.
  • Delays can happen during public holidays, Eid, bad weather or courier issues — we will keep you informed.
  • Please check the parcel in front of the delivery person before paying.
02

Return & Refund

Not satisfied? Return it easily — no stress, no hassle. You can request a return within 7 days of receiving your order.

You can return a product if

  • You received a wrong, damaged or defective product.
  • The product is unused and in its original box with all accessories.

We cannot accept a return if

  • The product is damaged by misuse, water or physical damage.
  • The product has been used, washed or its packaging is missing.
  • The return request is made after 7 days of delivery.

Refund process

After we receive and check the returned product, your refund is sent within 3–7 working days through bKash, Nagad or bank transfer. If the product was wrong or damaged, we also cover the return delivery charge.

03

Exchange Policy

Want a different size, colour or pack? You can request an exchange within 7 days of delivery, as long as the product is unused and in its original packaging.

  1. Call or WhatsApp us with your order number and the item you want instead.
  2. Send the product back to us, or hand it to our delivery partner.
  3. We ship the new item after checking the returned one. If the new item costs more, you pay the difference; if it costs less, we refund the difference.

Exchange delivery charges are paid by the customer, except when we sent a wrong or defective product.

04

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. We only collect what we need to deliver your order and improve your shopping experience.

Information we collect

  • Your name, phone number and delivery address when you place an order.
  • Your email address and message, if you contact us.
  • Basic browsing data such as device type and pages visited, to improve the website.

How we use it

  • To confirm, pack and deliver your order and to contact you about it.
  • To answer your questions and give customer support.
  • To tell you about offers, only if you choose to receive them.

Your information is safe

We never sell your personal information. We share only the details needed for delivery with our courier partners. Your cart and recent searches are saved only in your own browser.

05

Terms of Service

By using this website or placing an order with Ehans Mart, you agree to these terms.

  • Prices, offers and stock can change without notice. The price shown when you place the order is the price you pay.
  • Product images are for reference; colour may look slightly different on different screens.
  • We may cancel an order if the product is out of stock, the details are incorrect, or the order looks fraudulent. You will be informed by phone.
  • Please give a correct name, phone number and address. Orders that are refused at delivery without a valid reason may not be accepted again.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of Bangladesh.
