How Can We Help You?

Find quick answers about ordering, delivery, payment and returns. Can’t find your answer? Our team is here 24/7.

OrdersHow do I place an order?

Open the product, choose the option or pack you want and click Buy Now. Give your name, phone number and full address, then confirm the order. You can also call or WhatsApp us at +8801841650668 to order.

OrdersDo I need an account to order?

No. You can order as a guest — only your name, phone number and address are needed.

OrdersHow do I know my order is confirmed?

After you place an order, our team calls you to confirm the product, quantity and address before we dispatch the parcel. Please keep your phone on.

OrdersCan I change or cancel my order?

Yes, before the parcel is shipped. Just tell us on the confirmation call, or call / WhatsApp us as soon as possible.

DeliveryHow long does delivery take?

Inside Dhaka usually 1–2 days and outside Dhaka 2–4 days after your order is confirmed. Holidays or bad weather can cause small delays.

DeliveryHow much is the delivery charge?

Tk 80 inside Dhaka and Tk 130 outside Dhaka. Some bundle offers (like “Buy 3”) include free delivery.

DeliveryDo you deliver all over Bangladesh?

Yes. We deliver to all 64 districts through trusted courier partners.

DeliveryCan I check the product before paying?

Yes. Please open and check the parcel in front of the delivery person, then pay. If anything is wrong, tell us right away.

PaymentWhich payment methods do you accept?

We offer Cash on Delivery all over Bangladesh. You can also pay by bKash or Nagad if you prefer — ask our team on the confirmation call.

PaymentDo I have to pay in advance?

No advance payment is needed. You pay only when the product reaches your hands.

PaymentAre there any hidden charges?

No. You pay the product price plus the delivery charge shown at checkout — nothing else.

ReturnCan I return a product?

Yes. If the product is unused and in its original box, you can request a return within 7 days of delivery. Products damaged by misuse or water cannot be returned. Read the return policy →

ReturnI received a wrong or damaged product. What now?

Sorry about that! Call or WhatsApp us with your order number and a photo of the product. We will replace it or refund you, and we cover the return delivery charge.

ExchangeCan I exchange for another size or colour?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery if the product is unused. See how exchange works →

RefundHow long does a refund take?

After we receive and check the returned product, the refund is sent within 3–7 working days by bKash, Nagad or bank transfer.

ProductsAre your products quality checked?

Yes. Every product is checked by our team before it is packed and shipped from our hub in Rangpur.

ProductsWill the product look the same as the picture?

We use real product photos. The colour can look slightly different because of screen light and settings.

ProductsA product is out of stock. When will it be back?

Most products are restocked quickly. Message us on WhatsApp and we will let you know as soon as it is available.
