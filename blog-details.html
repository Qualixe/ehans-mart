7 Easy Ways To Baby-Proof Your Home Today

EM Ehans Mart Team Oct 15, 2026 6 min read

When your baby starts crawling, every drawer, fridge and door becomes an adventure. That curiosity is wonderful — but it also means small accidents can happen in seconds. The good news? You don’t need to change your whole home. These 7 simple steps keep your little one safe and give you peace of mind.

1. Lock the fridge and freezer

Toddlers love opening the fridge. Glass bottles, raw eggs and medicine stored inside can be dangerous, and an open door wastes electricity. A refrigerator child lock sticks on in seconds without any drilling, and adults can still open it easily with one hand.

2. Secure cabinets and drawers

Kitchen cabinets often hold cleaning liquids, knives and plastic bags. Use multi-functional cabinet locks on every low drawer and door your child can reach. Do a quick “crawl test” — get down to your baby’s height and see what they can grab.

  • Move cleaning products and medicines to a high shelf.
  • Lock drawers with sharp tools like knives and scissors.
  • Keep plastic bags and small items out of reach.

3. Stop doors from slamming

Little fingers get caught in closing doors more often than you think. An automatic door closer or a soft door stopper slows the door down, and door foam on the bottom gap also keeps out dust and mosquitoes.

4. Protect the head while learning to walk

Babies fall a lot between 8 and 18 months — it’s part of learning. A light, soft baby safety helmet protects the head from table corners and hard floors without making your baby uncomfortable.

“Most home accidents with small children happen in the kitchen and living room — exactly where we spend the most time together.”

5. Cover sharp corners and edges

Coffee tables, TV stands and bed frames are at the perfect height for a toddler’s head. Soft corner guards are cheap, easy to fit and remove the risk of painful bumps.

6. Keep travel time safe too

Safety doesn’t stop at the front door. On a bike or rickshaw, a baby riding safety belt keeps your child firmly attached to you, and a sun hat protects from the heat on sunny days.

7. Check your home every month

Your baby grows fast — what was out of reach last month may not be today. Walk around your home once a month, check every lock and stick-on item, and replace anything that has become loose.

Quick checklist

  • Fridge & freezer locked
  • Low cabinets and drawers locked
  • Doors can’t slam on fingers
  • Sharp corners covered
  • Medicines and cleaners stored high

Baby-proofing is not about saying “no” all day — it’s about creating a space where your child can explore freely and safely. Start with the kitchen today, and add one step each week.
